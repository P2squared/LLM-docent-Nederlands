# Dutch Word Bank — starter lists (NOT YET LEARNED)

> ⚠️ **These words are material to teach from. The learner does not know them yet.** Starter lists for **A1–B1**. From **B2** on, take vocabulary from authentic texts instead and record it in the tracker. When a word is taught in a session, copy it to `Dutch_Vocabulary.md` (the learner's own tracker). Never quiz a word from here as "review" unless it is in the tracker.

**How to teach from this bank** (see `Teaching_Best_Practices.md`):
- Teach **10–15 words per round**, each **in a sentence**, then test in context.
- Teach small abstract words as **contrast pairs** (al ↔ pas, nog ↔ weer), not from a table.
- Give each hard word a **hook**: word parts (op + halen), a sound-alike (*vacature* ≈ vacancy), or a picture.
- Always teach nouns **with their article**. **het**-words are marked; the rest are **de**.

## 2. High-frequency glue words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ook | also | *Ik kom ook.* |
| gewoon | just / normal | *U kunt gewoon binnenkomen.* |
| even | for a moment | *Wacht even.* |
| eigenlijk | actually | *Ik wilde eigenlijk thuisblijven.* |
| misschien | maybe | *Misschien kom ik later.* |
| meteen | immediately | *Bel meteen 112.* |
| bijvoorbeeld (bijv.) | for example | *U kunt bijvoorbeeld de bus nemen.* |
| zelfs | even (surprise) | *Het museum is zelfs op zondag open.* |
| daarom | therefore (+ verb next) | *…, daarom ben ik thuis gebleven.* |
| ongeveer | about / approximately | *De reis duurt ongeveer een uur.* |
| allebei | both | *Wij werken allebei.* |
| anders | different / otherwise | *iets anders* |
| tijdens | during | *tijdens de les* |
| zonder | without | *zonder afspraak* |

**Frequency scale:** nooit → soms → vaak → meestal → altijd

**Contrast pairs (teach together):**
| Pair | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **al ↔ pas** | already / early ↔ not until / late (*al om 8 uur* ↔ *pas om 12 uur*) |
| **nog ↔ weer** | still (didn't stop) ↔ again (came back) |
| **nog steeds ↔ niet meer** | still ↔ no longer |
| **eerst → daarna** | first → after that |
| **iemand ↔ niemand**, **iets ↔ niets** | someone ↔ no one, something ↔ nothing |

**Connectors:** en · maar · want · dus · of (normal word order) — omdat · als · dat · wanneer · toen · terwijl (verb at the end). See grammar 2A.2, 2A.3, 2A.6.

## 4. Prefix verbs (separable: the prefix goes to the end)

| Verb | Meaning | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| aanmelden / afmelden | sign up / cancel a sign-up | aan = towards, af = off |
| aanvragen · **de** aanvraag | apply for · application | aan + vragen (ask *for*) |

| afspreken · **de** afspraak | arrange to meet · appointment | af + spreken |

## 6. Common confusions (warn early)

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| wachten ≠ kijken | wait ≠ look |
| kosten ≠ duren | cost money ≠ take time |
| huis ≠ thuis | the building ≠ at home |
| komen ≠ koken | come ≠ cook |
| ontmoeten ≠ moeten | to meet ≠ must |
| worden ≠ woorden | to become ≠ words |
| **het** weer ≠ weer | the weather ≠ again |
| de data | dates (plural of *de datum*), not "data" |
| de werkgever ≠ de werknemer | employer ≠ employee |
