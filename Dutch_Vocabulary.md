# Dutch Vocabulary — the learner's own tracker

**Only words this learner has actually met** in a session. Starter lists to teach from live in `Dutch_Word_Bank.md` — a word moves here when it is taught.

Nouns are recorded WITH their article (de/het). De/het rules and the het-block live in `Dutch_Grammar_Rules.md` (1A.1, 2C.4).

**Method notes** (see `Teaching_Best_Practices.md`): teach small abstract words (pas, weer, zelfs…) as **contrast pairs in sentences**, not from tables; give hard words a **hook** (word parts, a sound-alike, a picture).
