# Inburgeringsexamen — Optional Module

Use this file **only if the learner is preparing for the Dutch civic integration exam** (inburgeringsexamen). Otherwise ignore it.

> ⚠️ Exam rules, routes and pass marks change. **Verify everything below on the official site (inburgeren.nl) and in the learner's own DUO account** before relying on it. The learner should confirm their route (e.g. A2 or B1) and exact exam dates, and record them in the profile.

---

## 1. Exam overview (A2 route, taken in the Netherlands)

| Part | Format | Time | Pass (approx., verify) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lezen** (reading) | 25 multiple-choice questions on everyday texts (letters, notices, ads, tables) | 65 min | ~18/25 |
| **Luisteren** (listening) | 25 multiple-choice questions on short audio/video fragments | 45 min | ~18/25 |
| **Schrijven** (writing) | 4 tasks: 1 form + 3 short texts (e-mail, note, short free text); pen & paper | 40 min | ~65% |
| **Spreken** (speaking) | Short spoken answers to video and picture prompts | ~35 min | — |
| **KNM** (knowledge of Dutch society) | ~40 multiple-choice questions: housing, work, income, health, education, government, norms | 45 min | ~70% |

- A paper **Van Dale NT2 dictionary** is allowed in **Lezen** and **Schrijven** only.
- **Official practice:** the practice exams on **inburgeren.nl**. Use these to calibrate: practice material written by Claude is useful, but usually easier than the real exam.

## 2. Lezen (reading)

**Format:** ~6–8 short functional texts, 2–5 questions each, options A–D.
**Text types:** appointment/announcement letters (doctor, landlord, gemeente) · temporary notices (route changes, closures) · tables (prices, schedules, courses) · posters with conditions · e-mails from school or work.
**Question types:**
1. **Main point**: "Waarom schrijft X deze brief?" (usually answered in the first lines)
2. **Match a constraint**: "X kan alleen na 15:00 — wanneer kan hij…?" (scan tables, dates, prices)
3. **What to do**: "Wat moet u doen als…?" (usually an imperative sentence: *Bel …, Vul … in*)

Wrong options are "close but wrong": real details from the text that don't answer the question.

**Method:** use the reading toolkit in `Dutch_Grammar_Rules.md` section **R** (question first → scan → read the whole phrase → evidence; long-sentence parsing; inference rules; periods and deadlines; unknown words).

**How to drill:** Claude writes short texts with A–D questions in this format. Start untimed with evidence required for every answer; then timed (~2.5 min per question); then full 25-question / 65-minute mocks. Calibrate with an official practice exam well before the exam date.

## 3. Schrijven (writing)

**Task types** (an exam mixes 4 of these):
- **Form**: personal details (name, address, postcode, phone, e-mail) + 2–3 open fields. Inventing realistic details is allowed.
- **Formal e-mail** to an organisation or teacher: usually 3 points (what / why / when or what you want). Open with *Geachte* or *Beste heer/mevrouw …*, close with *Met vriendelijke groet*. Use **u/uw** throughout.
- **Informal note or e-mail** (briefje) to a neighbour, friend or colleague: usually 3 things. *Hoi / Beste [naam]* … *Groetjes*.
- **Short free text** (e.g. for a neighbourhood paper) on a personal topic, guided by 3 questions.

**Typical situations:** reporting a problem to the gemeente · rescheduling or cancelling an appointment · a complaint (wrong bill, broken product) · asking for a day off or swapping a shift · borrowing and returning something · signing up for a course, library or volunteer work · asking a neighbour or colleague for help.

**Scoring order:** **completeness** (every point covered) → **comprehensibility** → language. A task that misses its points can score zero however good the Dutch is. **Clear and complete beats long and fancy.** Aim for a margin: one extra concrete detail per point.

**Proofreading checklist:** build a personal one from the learner's error history (60 seconds per task), e.g.: verb in 2nd position? · infinitive at the end after a modal? · correct form of *hebben/zijn*? · *u/uw* in formal mail? · opening and closing present? · the learner's top spelling words.

**How to drill:** rotate all task types; 10 minutes per task; mark completeness first; then a rewrite from memory to lock in corrections.

## 4. KNM (knowledge of Dutch society)

**Themes:** housing (renting, deposit, rent allowance) · work (contracts, probation, calling in sick, dismissal) · income (taxes, benefits, allowances) · health (GP first, referral for a specialist, health insurance is compulsory, deductible) · education (compulsory schooling, primary/secondary school) · government and society (laws, rights and duties, equality, freedom of opinion and religion, elections) · emergency services.

**Approach that works:** use KNM topics **as the reading texts** for Lezen practice. The learner then practises reading, learns KNM facts and builds exam vocabulary at the same time. Vocabulary per theme: `Dutch_Word_Bank.md` §5. Add a short fact quiz per theme closer to the exam.

## 5. Study plan template

Work backwards from the learner's exam dates (recorded in the profile).

| When | Focus |
|---|---|
| From day 1 | Shared foundations that serve every part: core grammar (word order, verb forms), high-frequency and trap words, KNM-themed reading texts, one short writing task per week |
| ~4 weeks before Lezen | Week 1: each text/question type, untimed, evidence for every answer · Week 2: tables, schedules, conditions + vocab · Week 3: timed sets · Week 4: full 65-minute mocks, including at least one **official** practice exam |
| ~3 weeks before KNM | Go through every theme with a short fact quiz; then mocks |
| Last 1–2 weeks before Schrijven | All task types under time; full 40-minute mocks; the personal proofreading checklist |
| Listening & speaking | Out of scope for text sessions. Advise ~15 minutes a day of easy listening (e.g. news in simple Dutch) from the start, because listening cannot be crammed. Register for exam slots early. |

Check the plan against the learner's real availability (holidays, travel) and move phases earlier if needed.
